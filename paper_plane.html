Trust protocol

No stored stories. No pretend-live mode.

  • No bundled story fixtures, no localStorage, no sessionStorage, no offline fallback.
  • A brand-new network request is made on page load, on every tab change, on every search, on reconnect, and every 60 seconds.
  • Every card shows its own publish time plus the exact fetch time and request number that produced it.
  • The Inspect raw live JSON link opens the exact endpoint your browser just called, so you can verify it yourself.
  • If a request fails or times out, the story area is cleared and the connection screen is shown. Nothing old is substituted.
About

Internet first. Offline never.

Paper Plane is a browser-based live reader. It does not ship a news database, story fixtures, or an offline story cache with the site.

When the live source cannot be reached, the site says so instead of presenting old content as current.
