How Paper Plane works

No stored stories. No pretend-live mode.

The story area is intentionally empty in the HTML. Every story shown there must arrive from a network request made by your browser. If that request fails, Paper Plane removes the results and shows the connection screen.

Return to the live desk →
Evidence rule

Source, timestamp and original link stay attached to every live result. Repeated stories can happen when the live source itself has not changed; they are never substituted from a local archive.

Live-only mode
About

Internet first. Offline never.

Paper Plane is a browser-based live reader. It does not ship a news database, story fixtures, or offline story cache with the site.

A fresh request is made on every page load, every tab change, every search, after reconnecting, and during scheduled live refreshes. If a live source cannot be reached, the site says so instead of presenting old content as current.
